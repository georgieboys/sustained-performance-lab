# AM1 - Adjunct Cognitive Reset Modalities

Sustained Performance Lab is built on the Gateway Protocol: a system of nervous-system reset, structural strength, metabolic conditioning, and long-term durability.

This section outlines **adjunct modalities for Cognitive Reset **— optional tools that support the core system by helping individuals arrive calmer, breathe better, and regulate more effectively. These modalities are not required and are not treatments. They simply create a low-friction environment for nervous-system downshift, somatic awareness, and recovery.

Some people will use many of these tools.

Some will use none,

but all will be used to collect data and make the base program better.

**Most of these adjunct modalities are currently specific to the Singapore SPL Dojo **

All modalities fall into two sensory pathways, each housed in its own space:

- Zero-Gravity Room — suspension, decompression, and weightlessness  
- Tatami Room — grounding, floor contact, and somatic settling

The technologies in both rooms are identical; only the posture differs. Some people settle best while suspended, others while grounded.

---

# I. Neuroacoustic & Sensory Modulation

### 1. Neuroacoustic Soundscapes (Endel, Binaural Beats)

Purpose: Cognitive quieting, reduced internal noise

Mechanism: Rhythmic auditory cues guide brain activity toward calmer, more coherent states

Evidence: Moderate for anxiety reduction and attention stabilization

Use: Headphones; recliner or tatami

---

### 2. Red Light Therapy & Liminal Lighting

Purpose: Visual calm, cellular support, recovery

Mechanism:

• Red/NIR light (630–660 nm, 810–850 nm) supports mitochondrial function

• Soft color lighting helps downshift the visual system

Evidence: Strong for tissue repair; moderate for mood and recovery

Use: Optional; both rooms

---

### 3. Vibroacoustic Pad (Low-Frequency Somatic Vibration)

Purpose: Deep somatic release and nervous-system softening

Mechanism: Low-frequency vibration transmitted through recliner or floor pad

Evidence: Emerging but consistently positive for relaxation and pain reduction

Use: Recliner or tatami

---

# II. Respiratory & Autonomic Regulation

### 4. Resistance-Based Breathwork Trainers

Purpose: Stronger diaphragm, improved breathing mechanics

Mechanism: Inspiratory/expiratory resistance builds respiratory muscle strength

Evidence: Strong in sports medicine and respiratory physiology

Use: Seated or reclined; short sessions (3–8 minutes)

---

### 5. CO₂ Tolerance & Breath Pacing Tools

Purpose: Vagal activation and stress resilience

Mechanism: Guided slow breathing (4–6 breaths/min) and controlled exhale training

Evidence: Strong for HRV improvement and anxiety reduction

Use: Recliner or tatami

---

### 6. HRV Biofeedback System

Purpose: Real-time autonomic self-regulation

Mechanism: Sensor-driven feedback guiding users into HRV coherence

Evidence: Strong; used in elite sport, military, and psychological resilience

Use: Short sessions (3–7 minutes); both rooms

---

# III. Neuroregulation & Cognitive Reset

### 7. Neurofeedback (EEG-Guided Self-Regulation)

Purpose: Attention training and cognitive downshift

Mechanism: EEG sensors provide real-time feedback during focused or calm states

Evidence: Strong in clinical populations; moderate in general use

Use: Optional; while reclined

---

### 8. Vagus Nerve Stimulation (tVNS)

Purpose: Gentle parasympathetic activation

Mechanism: Low-intensity stimulation at ear or neck

Evidence: Emerging but promising; safe at low settings

Use: Optional; during rest

---

# IV. Physical & Somatic Downshift

### 9. Zero-Gravity Recliner

Purpose: Musculoskeletal unloading and decompression

Mechanism: Neutral body posture reduces axial load and supports diaphragmatic expansion

Evidence: Strong ergonomic basis

Use: Core of the Zero-Gravity Room

---

### 10. Tatami Grounding Platform

Purpose: Floor-based somatic settling

Mechanism: Contact with firm, supportive surface enhances proprioception and rest

Evidence: Strong in somatic traditions; experiential

Use: Core of the Tatami Room

---

### 11. Compression Boots

Purpose: Circulatory support and recovery

Mechanism: Pneumatic cycles support venous return and reduce muscular fatigue

Evidence: Strong in athletic recovery

Use: Optional; in recliner

---

### 12. Gentle Inversion Table

Purpose: Spinal decompression and mild traction

Mechanism: Shallow inversion reduces lumbar loading and supports autonomic downshift

Evidence: Moderate; effects are user-dependent

Use: Separate corner; elective only

---

# V. Somatic Awareness Tools

### 13. Props, Bolsters, and Somatic Supports

Purpose: Comfortable resting shapes and gentle somatic exploration

Mechanism: Allow the body to release tension without strain

Evidence: Experiential; foundational in somatic modalities

Use: Tatami Room

---

# VI. Environmental Modulation

### 14. Ambient Sound & Nature Audio

Purpose: Background quieting and sensory safety

Evidence: Moderate

Use: Both rooms

---

### 15. Minimal Scent Integration

Purpose: Subtle mood shaping

Evidence: Mild but meaningful

Use: Optional; always subtle and never overpowering
