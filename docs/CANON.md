# CANON

The Canon is the unchanging backbone of SPL.

It defines what SPL is, how it works, and what principles are never negotiable.

Everything else—eventually, even future commercial formats—must orbit this document.

---

# 1. PURPOSE OF THE CANON

The Canon exists to guide humans toward:

- structural durability  
- cardiovascular resilience  
- metabolic stability  
- nervous system regulation  
- joint integrity  
- long-term functional freedom

The aim is not optimization.

Not peak performance.

Not aesthetics.

Not athletic superiority.

The aim is a body and nervous system that age well and resist breaking, regardless of wealth, environment, background, or access to equipment.

This is the floor of long-lived human performance—not the ceiling.

---

# 2. CANONICAL PRINCIPLES

These principles cannot be violated.

They hold SPL together across decades and geographies.

---

## A. Simplicity Above All

If a protocol becomes complicated, SPL removes or simplifies it.

Simple systems survive.

Complex systems drift.

The Canon stays simple so it remains:

- repeatable  
- teachable  
- universal  
- scalable  
- safe  
- durable

---

## B. Science Dictates Everything

The Canon updates when:

- strong evidence contradicts it  
- stronger evidence strengthens it  
- universal accessibility improves  
- metabolic, cardiovascular, or structural outcomes demand change

There is no guru doctrine.

No “SPL truth.”

Only the best available evidence, interpreted through durability and universality.

---

## C. The Universal Hackability Rule

Equipment is allowed only if:

1. the physiological effect is universal  
1. the tool is substitutable cheaply anywhere  
1. the principle survives even when the object changes

Examples:

- kettlebell → backpack of books  
- ice bath → bucket + ice  
- sauna → sun, heater, hot room, overdressing  
- sandbag → sack filled with rice or soil

Hackability ensures SPL does not depend on privilege.

---

## D. Accessibility Over Prestige

A person in a low-income environment must be able to follow the Canon.

If cost cannot stay affordable, the technique belongs in adjuncts—not the Canon.

---

## E. Removal Before Addition

If an element adds complexity, cognitive overhead, equipment dependency, or drift:

Remove it.

Subtraction keeps SPL clean.

---

## F. Honesty About Uncertainty

The Canon states clearly:

- what is strongly evidence-based  
- what is plausible  
- what is experimental  
- what is under review

This protects SPL from becoming pseudoscience wrapped in good branding.

---

## G. No Aesthetic Promises

The Canon does not care about appearance.

Bodies may change as a side effect.

But the goal is capacity and longevity.

---

## H. The Singapore Reference Lab Clause

Because SPL was born in Singapore, the Singapore Lab is:

- the reference implementation  
- the controlled testing ground  
- the environment where lab-grade tools (rowers, bikes) may be used in canonical protocols

But globally, SPL must remain independent of machines.
