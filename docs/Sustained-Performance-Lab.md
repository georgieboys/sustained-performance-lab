# Sustained Performance Lab

_A unified operating system for long-lived human performance._

This is an effort to answer the question: How should we maintain and strengthen the human body and mind so it performs better and ages well?

---

# OUR PURPOSE

Our aim is to build humans who can move through the world with:

1. Injury-resistant bodies that age well.  
1. Mentally agile and emotionally clear minds.  
1. Purpose and ability to effect change.

We work on long-term function, systems that work when life takes over. Systems that allow us to stay strong, calm, and capable for decades.

---

# KEY GUIDING PRINCIPLES

## Clarity before complexity

A calm mind learns better and trains better. Not everyone can meditate, but we all need tools to chill the fuck out.

## Injury prevention before strength

A stable frame prevents injuries and enables real progress. You need to give your tendons and ligaments time to catch up to your muscular strength.

## Sustainability > hyped-driven trends

It is more important to have consistent range of motion as you grow older than hitting all the cool metrics for a short period of time in your youth.

## Science over spectacle

Everything we use must have a plausible physiological grounding. Facts have no feelings. Placebo is fine, just don’t mistake it for science.

## Longevity over aesthetics

Looking good may happen, but it is a side-effect, not the north star.

## Fire-and-forget systems

Build routines that self-run instead of routines that need micromanagement. The systems must survive when life hits you with full force.

## Data over dogma

Challenge bullshit and let the data be your guide. Do what works for you, ignore all the noise.

## Obsession on life, not the tools

If you spend all your time worrying about health and how good you look, did you really live at all?

# THIS IS NOT A GYM

We are all guinea pigs here.

**Everyone who trains at SPL, including the people who built it, is part of the experiment.**

We run protocols, log what happens, and adjust. When something consistently works across different bodies and lives, it earns a place in our core system.

**No gurus. No secret hacks. Just curious humans, testing ideas in public.**

Our vibe is closer to an engineering lab than a bootcamp: small tweaks, slow progressions, no theatrics.

---

# THE CANON & THE DOJO

SPL lives in two layers: the stable core and the experimental edge.

## THE CANON

The Canon is our stable operating system. It’s the stack of practices that survived three filters:\
– makes biological sense\
– is backed by solid evidence\
– works in repeated real-world testing

The Canon changes when evidence or outcomes justify it.

## THE DOJO

The Dojo is our experimental floor.This is where we test things that are promising or interesting:\
– Neuroacoustic tools\
– HRV-guided training\
– vibration platforms, sensory rooms, breathwork toys

Tools are clearly labelled: proven, plausible, or mostly vibes.

---

# OPEN SOURCE PERFORMANCE

Built to be legible and shareable, not proprietary.

Written so anyone can read, question, and adapt to their own context. You don’t have to train with us in person to use the system. Treat this as an open lab notebook for building a body and brain that last.

**Not everything we touch has the same weight of evidence. That’s normal.**

Roughly, we sort tools into three buckets:

**Canon:** strong enough evidence + repeated success in practice\
**Dojo:** plausible and under active testing\
**On the Bench:** ideas we’re watching but not yet running

Placebo is allowed. Confusing placebo with mechanism is not. We’re comfortable saying “we don’t know yet,” and we label things accordingly
