SPL is built for human performance in regular life. And that means making sure you head is in the game (of life). That means learning how to use it well, controlling your ability to regulate your systems up and down without chemical pathways.

Most physical systems focus on _output_ (strength, endurance, skill). SPL focuses on **input quality** — the clarity of the nervous system that receives training.

**The Cognitive Reset = Master Input Filter**

Every adaptation — muscular, cardiovascular, connective tissue, behavioural — is gated by nervous system state.

The Cognitive Reset is therefore the **root protocol** of human change, by which a human gradually rewires their internal maps.

## THE PURPOSE OF THE COGNITIVE RESET

The Cognitive Reset helps your brain and body get into a state where you can learn, move, focus, and change more easily.

It clears mental clutter, breaks unhelpful thought loops, calms your system, improves how you respond, and makes it easier to remember, learn, and stay steady over time.

It’s not just a warm-up for training—it’s a warm-up for being a functional human.

Consistent practice builds the physiological and cognitive conditions under which long-term change becomes possible.

### Benefits of the Cognitive Reset:

- Reduce cognitive load and mental noise  
- Support state regulation and improve response flexibility  
- Facilitate learning and adaptation  
- Interrupt habitual cognitive patterns  
- Improve access to memory, perspective, and internal models  
- Enhance emotional regulation and long-term behavioural stability

## THE SIX ROUTES OF COGNITIVE RESET

Humans regulate in different ways. They think differently, respond to stress differently, and arrive in different internal states depending on the day, the hour, or even the minute.

- Some days you’re wired.  
- Some days you’re foggy.  
- Some days you’re emotional.  
- Some days your brain won’t shut up.  
- Some days your body wants to move before your mind can think.

Because our internal state keeps shifting, **one reset method will never work for everyone, every time**.

That’s why the Cognitive Reset gives you **six different ways in**:

- one for breath people,  
- one for movement people,  
- one for thinkers,  
- one for feelers,  
- one for social types,  
- one for visual minds.

Each method works on a different part of your nervous system.

Each one opens a slightly different door into calm, clarity, and readiness

The whole point is simple:

**Everyone gets a door they can walk through.**\
**Every day.**\
**In any state.**

People learn to choose the route that matches **their state**, not their mood. The key is not preference but effectiveness: the method that shifts the nervous system into a calmer, clearer, more stable mode is the right one.

The reset uses one (or more) of the following six neurobiological routes:

### [1. Autonomic Route — Breathwork](gp1-1-breathwork.md)

Works by shifting autonomic balance through slow, controlled respiration, especially extended exhales.

### [2. Cognitive Offloading Route — Brain Dump](gp1-2-brain-dump.md)

Reduces working memory load and mental clutter by externalizing thoughts onto paper.

### [3. Prefrontal Override Route — Pattern Interrupts](gp1-3-pattern-interrupts.md)

A short cognitive task that breaks repetitive thought patterns and restores attentional flexibility.

### [4. Co-Regulation Route — Short Conversations](gp1-4-short-conversations.md)

A short, human-to-human interaction that creates a shared regulatory field through presence, attention, and non-demanding conversation.

### [Creative Output Reset — Expression](gp1-5-expression.md)

Activates motor and executive networks, externalizes internal cognitive content, reduces working-memory load, and supports the re-establishment of cognitive clarity and direction.

### [6. Somatic Discharge Route — Kinetic Reset](gp1-6-deep-release.md)

A short sequence of passive, grounding positions that release neuromuscular tension and restore autonomic balance.
