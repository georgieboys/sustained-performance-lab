# (DRAFT ) 80/20 Nutrition Protocol

## 1. What is the 80–20 Nutrition Protocol?

The **SPL 80–20 Nutrition Protocol** is the base nutrition layer of Sustained Performance Lab.

Its job is:

> **Get you to a stable “B+” level of nutrition for decades, with the smallest number of actions per day.**

We are _not_ trying to:

- Make you a physique athlete.  
- Optimise every gram of carbs and fats.  
- Turn your entire life into a food project.

We _are_ trying to:

- Protect your **muscle**, **gut**, and **micronutrient status**.  
- Reduce long-term **cardiometabolic risk**.  
- Keep your **energy and cognition** stable enough to run the rest of SPL.

The philosophy is:

> **One big move + a couple of tiny anchors → then go live your life.**

People who _want_ extra optimisation and are willing to pay with time and attention are able to use this as a baseline go deeper, but the return vs effort ratio will be less optimal.

## 2. Before You Start: Triage & Archetypes

### 2.1 Health & Reality Triage

> **If you have serious medical issues, eating disorders, pregnancy/breastfeeding, or extreme food insecurity, the 80–20 Protocol becomes **_**adjacent support**_**, not the main driver.**

In those cases:

- Medical care, therapy, or structural issues come first.  
- SPL nutrition shifts into “gentle support mode” instead of aggressive changes.

### 2.2 Know What Kind of Eater You Are

SPL uses **eating archetypes** so that we can adjust _how_ you implement the protocol, without changing _what_ the protocol is.

These are the basic archetypes:

- **Controller** – loves rules, numbers, tracking.  
- **Soother** – uses food to calm emotions and stress.  
- **Reactor** – spontaneous, mood-based eating, very environment-sensitive.  
- **Environment-Driven** – eats whatever is nearby and easiest.  
- **Ghost** – forgets to eat / chronically under-eats / gets lost in work.  
- **Regulated / Intuitive** – basically stable, can self-correct without much structure.

In the **Archetype Deep Dive**, we map these to:

- CSIRO eater types  
- specific hacks for each archetype.

In the **main 80–20 Protocol**, the rule is:

> **Regardless of archetype, everyone runs the same core behaviour:**\
> **one Daily Stack + one or two Anchors.**\
> **Archetypes only change the “user interface”, not the physics.**

## 3. The Core Idea: One Heavy Stack + Simple Anchors

At the heart of the 80–20 Protocol is one big, simple move:

**You do ONE heavy nutrition “Stack” per day that handles most of your protein, fibre, and micronutrients in one sitting.**

Then you add:

- **1 protein anchor** (food-based)  
- **1 optional fruit anchor** (fun add-on, not mandatory but strongly encouraged)

That’s it.

The rest of your eating can be **normal life** – hawker food, family meals, office lunches, randomness.

---

## 4. The Daily Stack (The One Big Move)

### 4.1 What the Stack must do

The **Daily Stack** is a once-a-day event that hits three levers at the same time:

1. **Protein dose**  
1. **Fibre dose**  
1. **Micronutrient “insurance”**

We express the stack **per kilogram of bodyweight**, so it scales with size.

**Protein target per Stack:**

> **0.5–0.7 g of protein per kg of bodyweight in one sitting**

Examples:

- 50 kg adult → 25–35 g protein  
- 60 kg adult → 30–42 g protein  
- 75 kg adult → 37–52 g protein

This is the **main protein move** of the day.

**Fibre target per Stack:**

> **Around 10 g of fibre in that same sitting**\
> (roughly ~0.2 g/kg for many adults)

This gives you a big chunk of the SPL target of **~0.5–0.6 g/kg/day** fibre, without thinking about it all day.

**Micronutrient target per Stack:**

> **Take your once-daily micronutrient stack with the same meal/shake.**

This is usually a supplement (see Section 6).

We recommend this to be the first meal of the day and you can just chill and live and eat as normal for the rest of the day.

## 5. The Anchors: Protein & Fruit

After your Daily Stack, the 80–20 base only asks for **two simple anchors**.

### 5.1 Protein Anchor (Food)

> **One more food-based protein event later in the day.**

Target:

- **~0.3–0.4 g/kg protein** in that meal or snack

Examples:

- 50 kg adult → ~15–20 g  
- 75 kg adult → ~22–30 g

What it looks like:

- **Egg-based:**  
    - 3–4 eggs, or 2 eggs + cheese/yoghurt  
- **Fish-based:**  
    - one decently sized tin of tuna/sardines/mackerel  
    - or a fish portion at a regular meal  
- **Meat/poultry-based:**  
    - palm-sized piece of chicken, beef, pork, etc.  
    - hawker setting: choose the option where protein is clearly the main component  
- **Plant-based:**  
    - larger portion of tofu/tempeh  
    - lentil/bean-heavy bowl  
    - mixed grain + legume dish with enough legumes to be obviously protein-centric  
- **Busy/“Ghost” variant:**  
    - a simple second shake with ~25–30 g protein (no need to add fibre+micros again)

The job of the **Protein Anchor** is to:

- top up your protein for the day,  
- anchor at least one “real food” meal around protein,  
- let the rest of your food be whatever normal looks like.

---

### 5.2 Fruit Anchor (Fun, Light)

> **One “fruit anchor” sometime in the day.**

This is the **softest** part of the system, but powerful:

- adds some extra fibre,  
- adds polyphenols and micronutrients,  
- is easy to remember and execute.

Examples:

- 1–2 pieces of fruit  
- or equivalent in berries, cut fruit, etc.

In many environments, this is the simplest “upgrade”:

- swap one dessert / random snack → piece of fruit  
- or just _add_ fruit if total calories aren’t a concern

We will expand this in the **Fibre Stack Deep Dive**, but the main protocol just says:

> **Aim for one simple fruit moment most days.**

## 6. Micronutrient Activation (Supplements, in Tiers)

Most people will **not** get perfect micronutrient coverage from food diversity alone, especially in busy, urban, or lower-access environments.

The SPL stance:

> **For the 80–20 Protocol, we almost always recommend a once-daily micronutrient stack, taken with your Daily Stack.**

We keep it flexible by cost and availability:

### Tier 1 — Premium Convenience (All-in-One)

- One scoop of an “all-in-one” powder (AG1-type) that covers:  
    - multivitamin/mineral  
    - plus some phytonutrients, maybe probiotics, etc.

Your **micronutrient activation** is:

> _Take that with your Daily Stack shake._

### Tier 2 — Blueprint / Basic Stack

Separate, simpler components:

- 1 basic **multivitamin/mineral**  
- ± _Vitamin D_ (context-dependent)  
- ± _Magnesium_ in the evening (option)

Taken once per day = micronutrient activation.

### Tier 3 — Bare-Bones / Low-Access

- Cheapest reliable **generic multi** from a halfway decent manufacturer.  
- If even that is not available, we state clearly:

> “Micronutrient optimisation is limited by your environment. The main work is being done by protein + fibre.”

The deeper **Supplement Stack Deep Dive** will cover:

- exact SPL-recommended stacks by cost tier,  
- what Blueprint-style stack you’re using,  
- why some things matter more than others.

In the main article, the behaviour is simple:

> **Choose one micronutrient strategy that fits your budget and take it once daily with your Stack.**

## 7. Habit Changes & Swaps (How This Feels in Real Life)

The 80–20 base is not “add five new meals”.

It’s mostly **swaps and upgrades**:

- Instead of a large café latte in the morning →\
**coffee-based protein shake** that becomes your Daily Stack (coffee + protein + fibre + micros).  
- Instead of random snack later →\
**Protein Anchor meal** where you make the protein the star and let carbs/fats be whatever comes with it.  
- Instead of a third dessert in a week →\
**Fruit anchor** like an orange, apple, berries, or whatever is local and cheap.

Over time, these moves:

- raise your **protein and fibre floor**,  
- cover **micronutrients**,  
- leave you free to eat culturally normal food in every other slot.

### 4.2 What the Stack looks like in practice

You can do it as a **shake** or as a **solid meal**.

#### Option A — Shake Stack (most common)

One daily shake that:

- **Protein:**  
    - combines whey/soy/plant protein to hit your 0.5–0.7 g/kg protein target  
    - e.g. 40–50 g protein for many adults  
- **Fibre:**  
    - adds a mix like:  
        - ground flax + chia, or  
        - psyllium husk + flax/chia  
    - tuned once so the shake reliably gives ~10 g fibre  
- **Micros:**  
    - you take your multi / AG1-type / Blueprint basic stack at the same time

**Example (Singapore, 75 kg adult):**

- Protein:  
    - soy + whey combo tuned to ~45–50 g protein  
- Fibre:  
    - 1–2 tbsp ground flax + 1 tbsp chia to give ~10 g fibre  
- Micros:  
    - take your chosen once-daily micronutrient stack with this shake

You drink this once, ideally **early in the day**.\
You’ve just moved a huge chunk of your daily load in under 5 minutes.

We will have a **Protein Stack Deep Dive** later with:

- whey vs soy vs plant blends  
- collagen add-ons  
- cost tiers and local examples

The main article just says: **one Stack, tuned once, repeated daily.**

---

#### Option B — Solid Meal Stack

For people who hate shakes, the same Stack can be a plate:

- Protein:  
    - e.g. large omelette with cheese, plus yoghurt  
    - or a big portion of chicken/fish/tofu/lentils tuned to your 0.5–0.7 g/kg protein target  
- Fibre:  
    - load the meal with beans, lentils, veg, or add a fibre mix into yoghurt/oats  
- Micros:  
    - take your micronutrient stack with this meal

Again: **tune once, then repeat.**
