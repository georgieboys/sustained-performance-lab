# Physical Metrics That Matter

There are a million things you can track. We have chosen three metrics that give a reasonably clear picture of your health with cheap and widely available tests.

# Triglyceride : HDL Ratio

A simple, low-cost marker associated with metabolic and cardiovascular risk.

ApoB is the gold standard, but the ratio above is present in most blood tests.

[Deep Dive - TD:HGL vs ApoB](https://slite.com/api/public/notes/fMGbaOmAhOYxY8/redirect)

# VO₂max

The maximum amount of oxygen your body can use during intense exercise.

One of the strongest predictors of long-term health outcomes and easily trackable.

[Deep Dive - VO₂max](https://slite.com/api/public/notes/mAc-7_dq25clVK/redirect)

# Dead Hang Capacity

A simple way to test real-world grip strength and shoulder robustness.

Grip strength itself is one of the clearest predictors we have for frailty, independence, and even mortality.

[Deep Dive - Dead Hangs](https://slite.com/api/public/notes/hfbeThAK0q-A_2/redirect)
