# DOJO

## DOJO CHARTER — The Cultural Operating System of SPL

The Dojo is the experimental floor.

Where culture, identity, data, and custodianship live.

If the Canon is the skeleton,

the Dojo is the nervous system.

---

# 1. WHAT THE DOJO IS

The Dojo is:

- the testing ground  
- the place where protocols evolve  
- the community of serious practitioners  
- the birthplace of custodians  
- the home of collective intelligence  
- a laboratory for nervous system, movement, and recovery research

Not a gym.

Not a wellness room.

Not a casual drop-in space.

---

# 2. ENTRY PRINCIPLES

Entry is by reference only.

The Dojo accepts:

- people with curiosity  
- people with responsibility  
- people who regulate their own energy  
- people who want to contribute  
- people who want to learn and iterate  
- people who can handle feedback  
- people who are aligned with SPL philosophy

The Dojo rejects:

- drama carriers  
- fragile egos  
- optimization addicts  
- aesthetic chasers  
- pseudo-guru personalities  
- influencers seeking content  
- emotional volatility  
- people who drain energy  
- “fix me” tourists

The Dojo is for adults who want to build something.

---

# 3. CONTRIBUTION MODEL

The Dojo is pay-what-you-want, meaning:

Accepted contributions:

- time  
- labour  
- cleaning  
- data logging  
- note-taking  
- mentoring  
- helping maintain structure  
- operational support  
- facilities  
- or money

Unacceptable:

- entitlement  
- inconsistency  
- ghosting  
- taking without giving  
- treating the space as therapy  
- emotional dumping

Contribution is an ethic, not a transaction.

---

# 4. BEHAVIOUR & NORMS

Inside the Dojo:

- you show up honestly  
- you leave your ego outside  
- you don’t bring chaos  
- you respect time  
- you treat the space as a collective  
- you debate with curiosity  
- you accept correction  
- you don’t posture  
- you don’t recruit  
- you don’t evangelize

This is a high-trust, low-drama environment.

---

# 5. HIERARCHY

The Dojo is not democratic.

- The Founder defines the Canon  
    - Adds / subtracts after rigorous consultation with members.   
- Advisors guide direction.  
- Custodians maintain standards.  
- Senior members mentor juniors.  
- Juniors support the structure.

Robust debate is encouraged but once defined we follow the canon.

---

# 6. DATA CULTURE

By entering the Dojo, you consent to:

- anonymized data being logged  
- your results being used for system improvement  
- protocol refinements based on group outcomes  
- transparency on what works  
- documentation of experiments

This builds the public good.

This keeps SPL honest.

---

# 7. EXPULSION

A member is removed when they:

- violate norms  
- disrupt culture  
- drain energy  
- introduce pseudoscience   
- bring external drama  
- abuse the PWYW model

Removal is clean, quiet, and final.

---

# 8. EVOLUTION CLAUSE

The Dojo is where:

- new protocols are tested  
- adjuncts are explored  
- regressions and progressions emerge  
- VO₂max refinements evolve  
- thermal work is studied  
- recovery pathways are catalogued  
- knee and hinge variants develop

But:

- nothing becomes Canon without evidence  
- hackability cannot be violated  
- universality cannot be violated  
- simplicity cannot be violated  
- tendon-first principles remain sacred

The Dojo evolves.

The Canon stabilizes.
